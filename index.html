    Teach the wand a move

    1. Pick a slot and give it a name (or tap a suggested spell).
    2. Press Record. The wand ticks twice and buzzes "go": do the move once.
    3. Repeat 3 times. Change your grip between takes, it makes the wand more forgiving.
    4. Choose which IR code the move sends.

    Suggested moves

    Designed to work in any grip. Directions are from your point of view: "up" means up toward the ceiling, "right" means to your right while pointing at the TV.

    IR codes

    Two ways to fill a slot: Learn copies a button from your own remote (hold it near the wand's tip; works for air conditioners too), or Library picks a known code by device and brand.
